Arrival is not merge activity

The July merge spike reflects backlog consolidation. Reports existed beforehand and continued afterwards. The latest exported body says the reporter has stopped reporting these but a colleague encouraged this report; that is a reason not to interpret a quiet queue as recovery. This export cannot establish live service health, a shipped fix, or the number of unreported failures. The old page’s “fix queued” is not delivery evidence.

Unresolved symptoms and evidence boundary

The desk-noted bodies include a one-day shift, a June-only pattern, an invite arriving before booking, and different dates in Lisbon and Toronto. Those symptoms deserve follow-up with original timestamps, timezones, input values and reproduction steps. Tenant/channel classification files them under parser; it does not prove that the parser explains each body. Do not silently drop them, move them to hint, or open a separately counted timezone issue without evidence for that mechanism.
